# SEO & Backlink Strategy for ScanHindi.com

## 1. Domain Overview
- **Target Audience:** Students, Government Employees, Lawyers, Data Entry Operators.
- **Primary Keyword:** Hindi OCR, Image to Text Hindi, handwriting OCR Hindi.
- **Brand Name:** ScanHindi.com

---

## 2. Free Backlink Sources (High Authority Profile Creation)
Create profiles on these sites and include your link in the "Bio" or "Website" field:
1. **GitHub:** Create a repository for "Hindi OCR Documentation".
2. **Behance:** Upload a "UI Showcase" of ScanHindi.
3. **Linktree:** Use it as a landing page for social media.
4. **Medium:** Write a short article about "Digitizing Hindi Documents".
5. **About.me:** Create a personal brand profile.

---

## 3. Blog Commenting Sites (Niche: Hindi Tech/Education)
Search for these terms on Google: `"Leave a comment" + "Hindi typing"` or `"Notify me of follow-up comments" + "hindi ocr"`.
- **Target Blogs:** HindiMe.net, Deepawali.co.in, ShoutMeHindi.com.
- **Strategy:** Don't spam. Write a 3-4 sentence helpful comment about the blog post and mention how ScanHindi helps in a similar context.

---

## 4. Guest Posting Ideas
Pitch these topics to tech/education blogs:
1. "How AI is changing Devanagari script digitization in 2026."
2. "Top 5 tools for UPSC students to manage handwritten notes."
3. "The importance of Hindi OCR in Digital India initiatives."
4. "Step-by-step guide: Converting old family letters to digital text."

---

## 5. YouTube SEO Strategy
YouTube is the 2nd largest search engine.
- **Video Titles:**
  - Photo se Hindi text kaise nikale? (Hindi)
  - Best Free Hindi OCR Tool 2026 (English)
  - How to convert JPEG to Editable Hindi Text.
- **Action:** Put `https://ScanHindi.com` in the FIRST line of every video description.

---

## 6. Quora & Reddit Strategy
- **Quora Hubs:** "Hindi Language", "Artificial Intelligence", "Digital India".
- **Reddit:** r/india, r/hindi.
- **Method:** Search for "How to copy text from image" and answer with a tutorial. Only include the link if it naturally fits the answer.
